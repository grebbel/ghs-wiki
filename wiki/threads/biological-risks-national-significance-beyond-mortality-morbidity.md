# biological-risks-national-significance-beyond-mortality-morbidity

## Question

How are biological risks of national significance and do they impact society more than just mortality and morbidity?

## Why this thread matters

National risk decisions often prioritize deaths and clinical burden, but many biological events create wider disruptions in food systems, livelihoods, governance, trust, and service continuity.
