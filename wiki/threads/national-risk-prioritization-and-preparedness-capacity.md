# national-risk-prioritization-and-preparedness-capacity

## Question

How do nations decide in an ever-changing risk landscape which risks to prepare for and how to develop the required preparedness capacity?

## Why this thread matters

National preparedness is constrained by finite resources, changing hazard profiles, and uneven institutional capacity. Decision quality depends on how countries prioritize risk and convert assessment outputs into sustained capabilities.
